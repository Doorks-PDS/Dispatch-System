AI assistance across your operation

Meet Moses. More than a chatbot.

Moses is designed to work with authorized business records to support your team across daily operations. Available tools and action approvals depend on your workspace and configuration.

Dispatch & operationsLook up jobs, review work queues and clarify next steps.
Sales & estimatesFind past work, review scopes and prepare the next estimate for human pricing.
Purchasing & inventoryReview saved purchase orders, receiving records and catalog information.
AccountingReview invoice, supplier-bill and payment records where connected.
Customer communicationPrepare messages and document-delivery proposals for approval.
Business reportingRetrieve authorized record counts and operational status summaries.
Moses does not independently approve spending, issue invoices or send messages. Your team's permissions and approval process still apply. Ask during your demo which capabilities are enabled.
Ask Moses: “What needs my attention today?”

Get a record-backed summary of outstanding jobs, estimates, invoices, purchasing and incoming leads in your active workspace. Moses shows the source of each summary and asks for approval before supported actions.
